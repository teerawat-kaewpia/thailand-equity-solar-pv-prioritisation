# Data sources

Raw inputs are not redistributed here. They are public datasets with their own licences. This list is
Table S10 of the Supplementary Material, generated from the same build.

| Role | Dataset and provider | Version / native scale | Period / access | Use and processing | Access route / script |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 Resource | Global Solar Atlas v2, PVOUT long-term annual yield | v2, 1 km raster | Long-term average, files dated 2026-06-17 | Annual PV output resampled to 90 m by bilinear interpolation, 2nd-98th percentile normalisation at pixel level, then province mean rescaled once at province level. | globalsolaratlas.info, Scripts/02_Preprocessing/compute_c1_pvout_90m.py |
| C2 Equity | NESDC Poverty and Income Distribution, Table 1.5, NSO socioeconomic survey | Province statistic | 2022 (B.E. 2565), workbook dated 2026-07-12 | Provincial poverty incidence (%) rasterised to 90 m as the raw percentage, constant within each province, benefit direction. Clipped at the 2nd and 98th percentiles and scaled to [0, 1] once, at province level. | nesdc.go.th, Scripts/02_Preprocessing/rasterize_c2_poverty.py |
| C3 Demand | WorldPop unconstrained population | 2020 UN-adjusted, 100 m raster | 2020, downloaded 2026-06-15 (download log) | Population density resampled to 90 m and 2nd-98th percentile normalised at pixel level. The province mean is log-transformed, log(1 + 100x), then rescaled once at province level. | hub.worldpop.org, Scripts/02_Preprocessing/compute_c3_popdens_90m.py |
| C4 Infrastructure | OpenStreetMap power lines and cables (power=line, power=cable) | Vector network | OSM database state 2026-06-22 (Overpass timestamp) | Distance to the mapped power lines converted to an exponential proximity score with a 5 km scale, then zonally aggregated. | openstreetmap.org, Overpass API, preprocessing scripts in Scripts/02_Preprocessing |
| C5 Physical: forest | Hansen Global Forest Change, University of Maryland | v1.11 treecover2000, 30 m raster | Dataset release 2023, processed in GEE | Tree-cover constraint exported at 90 m and combined with terrain and built-environment components. | Earth Engine dataset catalog, Scripts/GEE/04_hansen_forest.py |
| C5 Physical: terrain | Copernicus DEM GLO-30 | GLO-30, 30 m raster | Tiles dated 2026-06-17 to 2026-06-21 | Slope derived from the DEM and represented as a terrain-suitability constraint on the 90 m grid. | copernicus.eu/dem, Scripts/GEE/05_copernicus_dem.py |
| C5 Physical: built environment | JRC GHSL GHS-BUILT-H ANBH | E2018 R2023A v1.0, 3 arc-second raster (about 90 m) | Reference epoch 2018, tiles dated 2026-07-03 | Built-environment constraint aligned to the 90 m analysis grid and combined into C5. | ghsl.jrc.ec.europa.eu, Scripts/02_Preprocessing/download_ghsl_building_height.py |
| Administrative aggregation | GADM administrative boundaries, Thailand | v4.1, province and district polygons | Downloaded 2026-06-15 (download log) | Province, amphoe and Bangkok district masks for zonal statistics, rankings and scale-sensitivity checks. | gadm.org, Data/01_Raw_Vector/gadm41_THA.gpkg |
| Solar deployment comparison | OpenStreetMap solar power plants | Point and polygon vector features | OSM database state 2026-06-22 (Overpass timestamp) | Overpass query for power=plant and plant:source=solar, used only for descriptive deployment comparison. | openstreetmap.org, Overpass API |
| Bangkok rooftop yield | Google Open Buildings footprint coverage | v3 polygons (Earth Engine asset GOOGLE/Research/open-buildings/v3/polygons) | Exported raster dated 2026-07-11 (GoogleOB_v3_density_30m_Bangkok.tif) | Footprint coverage rasterised and aggregated to the 50 khet, used for the district rooftop opportunity measure. | developers.google.com/open-buildings, Scripts/GEE/run_bangkok_density_export.py |
| Bangkok district equity | Bangkok Metropolitan Administration district administrative statistics | District statistics, 50 khet | Base year 2024 | Author-built vulnerability index from slum share, crowding, green deficit and disaster indicators, full construction in Section S8. | BMA administrative statistics, supplementary Section S8 |
| Provincial construct check | Author-built Thailand Provincial Socioeconomic and Financial Resilience Index (vulnerability FV and overall score SER) from NESDC/NSO provincial statistics | v1.0 weighted baseline model, 77 provinces (Thailand_Provincial_Index_2566_2567_GIS_v1.csv) | Reference period 2566–2567 (2023–2024), files dated 2026-07-12 | External comparison only (Table S13f), full construction in Section S8. It does not enter the PAI. | Supplementary Section S8, Scripts/06_Priority_Areas/validate_ser_external.py |
| Map base layer (main-text Fig. 2 only) | Natural Earth Admin 0 countries | v5.1.1, 1:10m vector | Downloaded 2026-06-15 | Neighbouring countries drawn as context in the study-region map. No analytical use. | naturalearthdata.com, DER_TEJ/Scripts/figures/fig02_study_area_tej.py |
| LV-density sensitivity only | GridFinder modelled low-voltage density (Arderne et al.) | gridfinder_lv_thailand.tif, 250 m raster (ESRI:54009) | Used for the Table S9 sensitivity | Normalised to [0,1] (2nd-98th percentile of non-zero cells) on the 90 m grid, province mean replaces C4 only in Table S9. Not a distance and not an MV layer. | GridFinder, Scripts/02_Preprocessing/compute_c5_grid_access_90m.py |
| Modelled MV distance (TEJ screen) | GridFinder modelled medium-voltage lines (Arderne et al.) | gridfinder_mv_lines_thailand.gpkg, 36,027 line features | Clipped 2026-06-23 | Euclidean distance from 90 m cells (EPSG:32647, cell centre inside the GADM 4.1 province union) to the nearest modelled MV line, province mean. A modelled network, not the utility network. | DER_TEJ/Scripts/mv_distance.py |
| Step 1 screen, excluded | VIIRS DNB annual night-time radiance, Thailand TGO grid emission factor | 2022 annual composite, raster | 2022 | Exploratory energy-related proxy, excluded because it overlaps with C3 demand density. It does not enter the PAI. Post hoc diagnostic in Section S13.11. | Earth Engine VIIRS catalog, TGO EFEC 2022, preprocessing records |

Policy documents are cited as agency documents. Their download links and dates are in
`data/tej/policy_sources/SOURCE_LINKS.csv`.
